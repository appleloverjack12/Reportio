Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Reporty ("the App"), operated by kajgod.agency, handles information when you use our Meta-connected reporting tool at reporty.netlify.app.

What the App Does

Reporty is an internal tool used by kajgod.agency to generate monthly social media performance reports for Facebook Pages and Instagram Business accounts that kajgod.agency manages on behalf of its clients.

What Data We Access

When you log in with Facebook, the App requests access to the following data through the Meta Graph API:

  • pages_show_list — the list of Facebook Pages you manage
  • read_insights — analytics and performance data for those Pages
  • pages_read_engagement — engagement metrics (posts, followers, reach) for those Pages
  • instagram_basic — basic profile information for linked Instagram Business accounts
  • instagram_manage_insights — analytics data for linked Instagram Business accounts

How We Use This Data

The data accessed through the Meta Graph API is used solely to:

  • Display monthly performance metrics (reach, impressions, followers, engagement) within the App
  • Generate branded PDF reports for kajgod.agency's clients

We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond generating the reports described above.

Data Storage

The App operates entirely in the browser. No data is stored on any server. All API responses are held in browser memory only and are cleared when the page is closed or refreshed. We do not maintain any database of Facebook or Instagram data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data obtained through Facebook or Instagram APIs with any third party. Data is never transferred outside of the immediate session used to generate a report.

Data Retention

Since no data is stored server-side, there is no retention. All data exists only in browser memory during an active session and is discarded immediately when the session ends.

Who Has Access

Only authorised kajgod.agency staff who manage client social media accounts use this App. The App is not publicly distributed and is intended for internal agency use only.

Facebook and Instagram Permissions

This App uses the Meta (Facebook) Login platform. Our use of data obtained through Facebook and Instagram APIs complies with the Meta Platform Policy and the Meta Terms of Service.

You can revoke the App's access to your Facebook account at any time by visiting facebook.com/settings → Apps and Websites and removing Reporty.

Cookies

The App uses the Facebook JavaScript SDK which may set cookies necessary for the Facebook Login flow to function. No other cookies are set by the App.

Children's Privacy

This App is intended for business use only and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top.
